## Summary

- **395 prompts** for the regular rounds: 35 easy (30+ answers), 155 medium (12–29) and 205 hard (5–11).
- **229** of them have a good spread: at least one obvious answer and at least two quiet stations to hunt for.
- **1491 Interchange prompts** for the double-points final round (two conditions at once, 3–12 answers).
- At 6 regular prompts a day, that's **65 days** before a regular prompt repeats.

A prompt is kept if it has 5–100 answers (some families have a lower maximum) and shares less than 80% of its answers with an earlier prompt.
Families of near-identical prompts, like walking distance or stops away, use a stricter limit within the family.
Answers are listed busiest station first, which is roughly the order players will think of them.
